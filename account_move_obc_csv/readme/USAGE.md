After the month is closed, go to *Accounting \> Accounting \> Closing \>
OBC Exports*, create a record with the period (the previous month by
default), and click *Export CSV*.

The export takes the posted journal entries of the period that no export
has taken yet, and downloads the file. Clicking *Export CSV* again
downloads the same entries again.

An exported journal entry cannot be reset to draft. To export a period
again, an accounting administrator deletes the export record, which
releases its journal entries.
