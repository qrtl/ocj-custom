Customer-level billing conditions on partners, read by billing logic as
defaults:

- **Prorated billing** (日割り請求) and, under it, whether the installation
  month and the collection month are prorated (開始月に適用／回収月に適用).
  All three are on by default: proration is the rule and the customers
  billed in full are the exceptions. The month settings are separate because
  a contract can prorate one end and not the other.
- **Billing scope** (全請求／部分請求), on the purchase side: the vendor
  contract condition for rented equipment, on whether the vendor is paid
  regardless of the patient's visits.

All of them sync from the company to its contacts and are read-only there.
