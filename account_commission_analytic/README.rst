This module gives commission bills the analytic distribution of the
customer invoices they settle, so that a commission is charged to the
same cost centers as the sales it was earned on.

When the settled invoice lines carry different analytic distributions,
the settlement is billed as one line per distribution. Lines whose
invoice line has no analytic distribution are billed on a line without
one, which the analytic distribution models fill as on any other bill
line.
