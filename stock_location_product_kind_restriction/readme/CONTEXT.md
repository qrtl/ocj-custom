At a branch where each person keeps the equipment they are responsible for in a
location of their own, while consumables are kept in a shared location of the
branch, equipment and consumables must not end up in each other's locations.
Accessories go with the equipment, including those without a serial number, so
the check cannot rely on how a product is tracked.

Setting the branch's stock location to **Equipment and Accessories Only**
and its shared consumables location to **Consumables Only** keeps consumables
out of the personal locations, including ones created later, and equipment out
of the shared one.
