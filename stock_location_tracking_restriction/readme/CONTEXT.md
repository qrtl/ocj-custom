At a site where each person keeps the equipment they are responsible for in a
location of their own, every unit identified by its serial number, while
consumables are shared in the other locations of the site, equipment and
consumables must not end up in each other's locations.

Setting the site's stock location to **Quantity-Tracked Only** and each
person's location under it to **Serial- or Lot-Tracked Only** keeps equipment
out of the shared locations, including ones created later, and consumables out
of the personal ones.
