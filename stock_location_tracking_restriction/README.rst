This module limits the products a stock location accepts by whether they
are tracked by serial or lot number. A location can be set to accept:

-  **Serial- or Lot-Tracked Only**: products tracked by serial or lot
   number;
-  **Quantity-Tracked Only**: products tracked by quantity only, with
   neither serial nor lot numbers.

Validating a transfer or applying an inventory adjustment that would put
any other product in the location is refused, with the products and the
location named in the error. Editing the destination of a done move line
to such a location is refused the same way.

The setting applies to the location and to its sublocations; a
sublocation with its own setting follows that setting instead. It only
checks stock coming in: stock already in the location when the setting
is made is left as it is, and can still be moved out.

A typical use is a site where each person keeps the equipment they are
responsible for in a location of their own, every unit identified by its
serial number, while consumables are shared in the other locations of
the site. Setting the site's stock location to **Quantity-Tracked Only**
and each person's location under it to **Serial- or Lot-Tracked Only**
keeps equipment out of the shared locations, including ones created
later, and consumables out of the personal ones.

Configuration
=============

1. Go to *Inventory > Configuration > Locations* and open the location.
2. Set **Accepted Products**. The field is shown for internal and
   virtual locations; set it on a virtual location to restrict all the
   locations under it.
3. Leave the field empty to accept any product, or to follow the setting
   of a parent location.

The **Accepted Products** column can be shown in the location list from
the optional columns.
