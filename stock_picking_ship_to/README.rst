This module adds a **ship-to name, address and phone** to transfers, as
plain text, for destinations that are not partners in Odoo (for example
a patient's home given by an external system with each order) and for
systems that take the destination as text.

The fields default from the transfer's partner and stay editable:

- **name**: the partner's name, or for a contact, its company and its
  name joined by a full-width space;
- **address**: state, city, street and street 2 on one line, in Japanese
  order;
- **phone**: the partner's phone.

A value given when the transfer is created, or written later, is kept
until the partner changes.

Usage
=====

Open a delivery order: the **Ship-to Name**, **Ship-to Address** and
**Ship-to Phone** fields are under the partner. They are filled from the
partner when it is set; change them when the goods go somewhere else.
